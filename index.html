Questions

Answers before you ask.

The three we hear most often. Anything else — ask us directly above.

How much does an engagement cost?

Fixed fees, quoted in writing before any work begins. Individual returns start at $450, corporate engagements at $2,400 a year. You will never see an hourly invoice you did not agree to in advance.

Do you work with clients outside New York?

Yes. We are licensed to practise before the IRS in all 50 states, and most of our work runs remotely through encrypted document exchange. Multi-state and expatriate filings are a routine part of the practice.

How accurate is the tax estimator?

It applies current federal brackets and the standard deduction, so it is a solid directional figure — but it does not model state tax, credits, or itemised deductions. Treat it as a starting point, not a filing position.
